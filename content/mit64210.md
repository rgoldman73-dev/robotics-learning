## 8
title: Clutter: contact simulation

read: manipulation.csail.mit.edu chapter "Bin Picking" (simulation)
Q: What does a friction cone describe? => The set of contact forces that do not slip, |f_t| <= mu f_n.
Q: Why is contact hard to simulate? => It is non-smooth (impacts, stick/slip), stiff, and sensitive to geometry.
X: Drop 10 objects into a bin in simulation and observe the result for different friction values.
R: Clutter means many contacts; simulation of contact is a field of its own.
## 9
title: Clutter: grasp selection
topics: Antipodal grasps, grasp metrics, sampling grasp candidates from point clouds
read: manipulation.csail.mit.edu chapter "Bin Picking" (grasp selection)
Q: What is an antipodal grasp? => Two contacts whose normals oppose each other along the line between them, so forces can balance within friction cones.
Q: How are grasps chosen in clutter? => Sample many candidates from the point cloud, score them with a cost (alignment, collision, normals) and pick the best.
X: Write a scoring function for a parallel-jaw grasp given two contact points and normals.
R: Grasp selection = sample + score + collision check.
